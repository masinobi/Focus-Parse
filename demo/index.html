    Audio-visual pacing

    The highlight is not interpolated. Each sentence is spoken from the current word's offset, and the synthesizer's own boundary events are mapped back to a word — so the caret sits on what you are hearing, not near it.

    Marked by the document

    A recovered table and a blanked term already have exactly one right answer in the parsed text. An answer the app can mark itself is an answer it can demand — no model, no key, no network.

    Split channels

    In prose the eye gets a short badge and the ear gets the term in full: eCRF is spoken as "electronic case report form". Reading four letters teaches nothing. A grid card is the exception — it shows one cell alone and the question that follows draws its answer from that same cell, so there the two channels say exactly the same thing.

    Nothing is assumed

    Every other mechanism assumes a reader is there. The presence check is the only one that verifies it, and it is the reason a session's record means something.

    Demo pacing is accelerated. In the real app a presence check comes every 90–135 seconds, a spot check every 250 words, and an intercept at each section boundary; here they are tuned to fire within a short visit. Speech uses your browser's own synthesizer — where it reports no word boundaries, the caret falls back to an estimator and says so. A blank is never cut from a cross-reference: "Section ____ defines the review cycle" asks where something is, not what it says, so the check skips it and takes the next candidate instead.
